# About Me

I'm a full-stack developer and Software Engineering student at PUC Minas, working mostly with TypeScript, Next.js and PostgreSQL.

Most of what I know came from building **[FluxoJR](https://github.com/DevGentil/FluxoJR)** — a financial system for a holding of clinics that tracks cash flow across units and works out what each doctor gets paid. Its rules came from the spreadsheets the business actually used, so the interesting part was never the CRUD. It was making a contract's value a sequence of rates over time instead of a single number, keeping calendar dates from sliding a whole month in UTC-3, and making sure a consolidated view never sums a unit the account isn't allowed to see. Next.js 16, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Supabase Auth — and 496 tests around the rules that move money.

What I care about: business rules that live in one tested place, permission checks that can't be skipped by accident, and code that explains why it does what it does.
